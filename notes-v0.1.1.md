First release of **Mr. Rescue** for the Miyoo Mini Plus (OnionOS). This is an **unofficial port** of [Mr. Rescue](https://github.com/SimonLarsen/mrrescue) by Tangram Games. Please report problems [here](https://github.com/snyderman3000/mrrescue-mm-/issues), not to Tangram Games.

**Install:** copy the `Roms` folder from the zip to the root of the SD card (or install it from Mixtape), then launch **Mr. Rescue** from Games › Ports › Platform.

- The full game runs at full speed, with sound, the lighting effects and saved high scores.
- Handheld controls: **B** jump, **A** grab/throw, **Y/X/L1/R1** shoot (hold Up or Down to aim), **Start** pause, **Select+Start** quit. In menus, A is OK and B is back.
- **Options › Screen:** SHARP (crisp 2× pixels), FIT (full height) or STRETCH (whole screen).
- Fixed since the 0.1.0 test build: the game no longer crashes when you quit.
